# InvestIQ — An AI-Powered Deep Research and Intelligent Financial Analysis Platform
## Overview

InvestIQ is an AI-powered financial research platform designed to simplify market analysis for retail investors. It brings together real-time market data, fundamental analysis, technical indicators, sentiment analysis, and market trends in one place.

The platform helps users understand complex financial information through simple **BUY, HOLD, or SELL insights** for educational and research purposes.

## Features

* Real-time stock market data and price tracking
* Fundamental and technical analysis
* Sentiment and market trend analysis
* BUY, HOLD, and SELL insights
* F&O analysis using CPR, Pivot Points, and Camarilla levels
* IPO and mutual fund research
* Cryptocurrency and forex market tracking
* NAV tracking
* Trending stocks and risk assessment
* Event-based market alerts

## Problem Statement

Retail investors often need to use multiple platforms to research stocks, IPOs, mutual funds, and market movements. This process can be time-consuming and difficult, especially for beginners.

InvestIQ solves this problem by providing multiple financial research tools and market insights through a single, organized platform.

## Tech Stack

React.js, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Python, REST APIs, Tailwind CSS, GitHub, Postman

## Disclaimer

InvestIQ is developed strictly for **educational and research purposes**. It does not provide SEBI-registered investment advisory services, and users should not consider the platform's insights as financial advice.
