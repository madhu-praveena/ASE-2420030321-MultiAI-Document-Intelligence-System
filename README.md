# InvestIQ — Deep Research for Indian Stocks

> An AI-powered financial research platform designed to simplify and centralize market analysis for retail investors.

## About the Project

InvestIQ is a financial research platform that brings together market data, fundamental analysis, technical indicators, sentiment analysis, and market trends in one place.

The project aims to simplify complex financial information and provide easy-to-understand research insights such as BUY, HOLD, or SELL. It also supports research related to stocks, F&O, IPOs, mutual funds, cryptocurrencies, and forex.

**Disclaimer:** InvestIQ is developed strictly for educational and research purposes and does not provide SEBI-registered investment advisory services.

---

## Problem Statement

Retail investors often rely on multiple platforms to analyze stocks, track market movements, research IPOs, and evaluate mutual funds. This makes financial research time-consuming and difficult, especially for users without advanced financial knowledge.

InvestIQ addresses this problem by providing a unified platform that centralizes financial data and research tools.

---

## Key Features

* Real-time market data tracking
* Fundamental analysis
* Technical analysis
* Sentiment and market trend analysis
* AI-assisted BUY, HOLD, and SELL insights
* F&O analysis using CPR, Pivot Points, and Camarilla levels
* IPO and mutual fund research
* Cryptocurrency and forex tracking
* Risk assessment
* Trending stocks and market updates
* NAV tracking
* Event-based alerts
